🎓 TrackFlow: SE Evaluation Study Guide

Evaluation Rule: "Marks will be awarded for applied understanding, not memorized definitions."

1. Problem, Stakeholders & Mini SRS

Problem: Shopify store owners lose money when customers provide wrong phone numbers or are not available for delivery (RTO - Return to Origin). Manual confirmation calls are slow and expensive.

Stakeholders: Vendor (Admin) and Customer.

Strong Answer: "Sir, our Mini-SRS defined the boundary of the system. We focused on the **Functional Requirement** of order verification. Instead of just writing it down, we implemented a Shopify Webhook in routes/webhooks.js to automate this process."

2. Architecture, Coupling & Cohesion

Architecture: 3-Tier Architecture (Presentation, Logic, Data).

Proof of High Cohesion: "Our services/trackingService.js is Highly Cohesive because it only handles Courier API logic. It doesn't know anything about Shopify or UI."

Proof of Low Coupling: "The Shopify flow and the Tracking flow are Loosely Coupled. They share a database but their code is independent. Changing the Courier API does not break the Shopify Webhook."

3. MVP & MoSCoW Prioritization

MVP: The smallest useful version that sends WhatsApp alerts.

Strong Answer: "We used MoSCoW to prevent Scope Creep. We moved 'Multi-language support' to the 'Could-Have' category to ensure our 'Must-Have' (WhatsApp Buttons) was perfectly tested for the MVP."

4. Business Rules to DB Mapping

Proof: "Our Business Rule was: 'Only mark as confirmed if the user says YES'. We mapped this to an Enum in our Prisma Schema (PENDING, CONFIRMED, CANCELLED). This ensures data integrity at the database level."

5. Requirements Validation (Security)

Proof: "We validated our security requirements by implementing HMAC Signature Verification. In routes/webhooks.js, we calculate a SHA256 hash to prove the order really came from Shopify and not a hacker."

6. UX & Product Thinking

Proof: "We designed the UX for Zero Friction. The customer confirms with one click in WhatsApp. This high UX clarity is the core value of our project."
